\title{Historical recalibration versus additional reference checks in CO sensor arrays: a chronological evaluation}
\input{author_details.tex}
\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
Reference-assisted calibration must distinguish historical correction from the value of additional checks. A chronological CO sensor-array evaluation uses established correction rules in three development sessions and a separately frozen temporal confirmation on three later sessions, with twelve correlated peer configurations and two sensor families. Predictions precede reference purchase. Frozen confirmation benefit requires lower strict 1 ppm exceedance and nonpositive mean absolute error (MAE) change pooled and in both families. In development, historical affine correction reduces pooled random forest MAE from 0.8993 to 0.7843 ppm, while all fifteen affine comparisons with 20 additional checks per session have adverse pooled MAE changes; lower-budget and upstream-model sensitivities differ. In confirmation, each session independently resets to the same D10 initialization. The prespecified envelope-driven 20-check schedule reduces pooled affine B0 MAE from 1.3553 to 1.3257 ppm and strict 1 ppm exceedance from 43.06\% to 42.14\%, meeting the incremental aggregate criterion. This reverses the development pattern. Historical benefit remains heterogeneous: FIS MAE worsens relative to unchanged consensus. Incremental FIS MAE improves from 0.9471 to 0.9033 ppm but remains worse than unchanged consensus at 0.8820 ppm. Adverse D12 and FIS D13 cells preclude uniform-session improvement, and envelope-schedule superiority is unsupported. Conditional resampling is development-only; no confirmation bootstrap was performed. Confirmation midpoint-envelope diagnostics record 284 raw noncontainments among 3,600 outputs and, separately, 38 false assurances among 74 flagged outputs and 38 among 3,600 total outputs. These diagnostics are not affine uncertainty intervals. The contribution is an information-matched evaluation of established algorithms; both stages use one apparatus and establish neither independent-array generalization nor certified uncertainty.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Data and code availability}
The original measurements are public from UCI under DOI \href{https://doi.org/10.24432/C5S302}{10.24432/C5S302} \cite{uci487}. Full-precision development and separately frozen temporal-confirmation predictions, query masks, causal access logs, protocols, numerical endpoints and their source hashes are retained in a portable local reproducibility archive with original training/replay code and permitted development feature inputs. The accompanying numerical files provide full-precision inputs for the included tables and figures. A public code/prediction deposit and release identifier are not yet available; the final access and licensing arrangement awaits author approval. R7/R14 and the UCI270 reserved batches remain excluded.

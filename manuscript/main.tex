\title{Historical recalibration versus additional reference checks in CO sensor arrays: a chronological evaluation}
\input{author_details.tex}
\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
Reference-assisted calibration should separate gains from historical recalibration from gains due to newly acquired references. We evaluate this distinction using public temperature-modulated CO sensor measurements from two families. Chronological development and a frozen evaluation each contain 300 chamber exposures and twelve correlated peer configurations per exposure. Predictions precede reference revelation, and budgets of 0, 5, 10 or 20 checks per session are matched across correction methods and schedules. In development, historical inverse affine correction reduces pooled random-forest mean absolute error (MAE) from 0.8993 to 0.7843 ppm, while all fifteen affine comparisons at 20 checks worsen pooled MAE relative to their own zero-check baselines. In the later-session evaluation, the prespecified envelope-driven 20-check comparison instead reduces pooled affine MAE from 1.3553 to 1.3257 ppm and strict 1 ppm exceedance from 43.06\% to 42.14\%, satisfying the frozen aggregate criterion in both families. This aggregate gain is dominated by D11: pooled MAE improves by 0.0918 ppm on D11, worsens by 0.0049 ppm on D12, and changes by only 0.0021 ppm on D13. FIS improves relative to affine B0 but remains worse than unchanged consensus. Residual-envelope assurance is also unstable: 74 of 3,600 confirmation outputs receive a 1 ppm midpoint assurance flag, and 38 flags are false, with 37 occurring on D11. The results support separation of historical, incremental and schedule effects, without implying uniform improvement, independent-array generalization or traceable uncertainty.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Data and code availability}
The source measurements are publicly available as \emph{Gas sensor array temperature modulation} in the UCI Machine Learning Repository under DOI \href{https://doi.org/10.24432/C5S302}{10.24432/C5S302} \cite{uci487}. Code, frozen numerical evidence and reproduction scripts for the reported development and temporal-confirmation analyses are deposited at \url{https://github.com/asayghe1/CO-Sensor-Reference-Calibration}. The raw UCI archive is not redistributed; it can be obtained directly from UCI using the documented download instructions. R7/R14 raw data and the sealed UCI270 batches are not part of this study release.

\section{Numerical provenance and denominators}
Values derive from authentic saved development prediction arrays and causal replays. The reproducibility archive records versioned SHA256 protocol and source manifests. Development historical calibration is D5/D7/D9, assessment D6/D8/D10, and all original fit/exclusion/reservation roles remain fixed. Each assessment has 100 shared exposures and 1,200 correlated configuration outputs, with six configurations per family. No reserved measurements enter these development results; the fixed-D10 temporal-confirmation evidence is reported in S10.

Original B0 copies are collapsed only after exact equality of predictions, references, query flags and intervals is checked. The original logs and removed-duplicate mapping remain intact. Three affine lambdas retain named identities but share one B0 numerical state. Every original bound/upstream sensitivity is retained in the CSV, even where only primary results are typeset. Each numerical row has complete model/bounds/schedule/budget/seed/day identity and source provenance; tables show four-decimal MAE and percent rates, while CSVs retain stored precision.

Random seeds 487--496 are averaged within each day before equal-day means. Seeds and overlapping peer configurations do not add physical replication. All/queried/unqueried supports and their own-subset consensus/B0 are retained. Empty queried B0 cells and unsupported bands have undefined rates, not zero. The 10\% relative sensitivity uses a strict exceedance and absolute 1 ppm at reference zero.

\section{Development-only conditional resampling and temporal structure}
No confirmation bootstrap was performed. For development D6/D8/D10 only, paired overlapping blocks use 2,000 replicates, seed 487 and fixed lengths 5/10/20. One index bank per length/day is shared across all arms/families/endpoints. Configurations are not resampled independently. Fixed fits, trajectories, initialization and shared reference labels are conditioned upon; learning and reference uncertainty are not resimulated. Percentile intervals are conditional sensitivity bounds, requiring regularity assumptions for an inferential interpretation. Adaptive chronological loss paths may be nonstationary. Neither a zero crossing nor an excluded zero establishes equivalence, causal drift explanation or independent-array coverage. The complete CSV retains every comparator/metric, all three session effects and direction counts. Quarter means, lag-one correlations and linear trends inspect temporal structure without validating arbitrary nonstationarity.

\section{Fixed-D10 temporal confirmation}\label{sec:sc5}
These are the frozen D11/D12/D13 temporal-confirmation results, not additional development folds. Upstream RF fitting uses D1,D2,D4--D9 only; correction calibration and controller normalization use D10 only. Every session, policy, budget and seed resets to that same initialized state. R7/R14 and UCI270 batches 7--10 remain excluded. All 129 final paths were committed before summaries, and saved-output recomputation reproduces all 40,635 run metric rows and 5,040 aggregates. The protocol/source/model hashes and development evidence remain unchanged; no refit, retuning or final-result method selection occurred.

The original pooled primary contrasts are historical affine $\lambda=1$ B0 minus raw consensus and incremental affine $\lambda=1$ envelope B20 minus own B0. The frozen descriptive criterion requires lower strict 1 ppm exceedance and nonpositive MAE change pooled and separately in both families; strict exceedance is primary and MAE is the guardrail. Historical benefit is heterogeneous because FIS MAE worsens on all three days. Incremental benefit meets the pooled-and-both-family aggregate criterion, but session reversals preclude uniform improvement. Envelope superiority over all three original schedule comparators is unsupported. FIS MAE improves relative to affine B0 (0.9471 to 0.9033 ppm) but remains worse than unchanged consensus (0.8820 ppm). The full cell tables retain all unfavorable values.
